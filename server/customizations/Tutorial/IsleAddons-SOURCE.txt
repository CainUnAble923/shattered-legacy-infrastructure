Wakening Isle addons (cc-P724, TutorialIsleAddons.cs): credits and terms

Each is a ServUO/RunUO community script from servuo.dev, downloaded 2026-10-28 at Chase's request and kept for
reference (never built or run from there) in D:\UO\third-party\code\<folder>. Ported to ModernUO here: the same
stock-art pieces at the same offsets, as Wakening Isle decoration. No terms were stated for any of them; as with the
other community work we use, the authors are credited, the work ships only inside the private repo and the shard, and
it is not reposted. All four use stock Ultima Online art only: no client change.

- Shipwreck (the west-facing one) by Celestial: "Shipwrecks! East, West and North facing".
  https://www.servuo.dev/archive/shipwrecks-east-west-and-north-facing.1123/ (folder celestial-shipwrecks)
  Ported: WakeningIsleShipwreckAddon, 20 pieces from ShipwreckWest_Addon.cs.

- Lighthouse by Milva: "New Lighthouse".
  https://www.servuo.dev/archive/new-lighthouse.157/ (folder new-lighthouse)
  Ported: WakeningIsleLighthouseAddon, 3 pieces from NewLighthouseAddon.cs.

- Moongate frame by Carl Hamilton: "MoongateStructure ADDON".
  https://www.servuo.dev/archive/moongatestructure-addon.2275/ (folder moongate-structure)
  Ported: WakeningIsleMoongateFrameAddon, 88 pieces and 3 lanterns from MoongateStructure.cs.

- Brass fountain by Lagatha: "New Fountions".
  https://www.servuo.dev/archive/new-fountions.1516/ (folder lagatha-brass-fountains)
