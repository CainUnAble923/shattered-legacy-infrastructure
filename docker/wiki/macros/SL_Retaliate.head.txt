====== Retaliate macro (SL_Retaliate.py) ======

Runs in the background next to whatever else you are doing (mining, for example). When you take damage, or a monster gets right next to you, it attacks the nearest hostile, bandages if you drop low, and walks you back to where you were standing when the fight is over.

By default it only fights monsters, never other players, and never anyone on your friends list.

===== Settings worth knowing =====

^ Setting ^ Default ^ What it does ^
| ''SCAN_RANGE'' | 6 | How far out it looks for attackers. |
| ''CHASE_LIMIT'' | 8 | Never chases further than this from your spot. |
| ''RETURN_HOME'' | True | Walk back to your spot after the fight. |
| ''ATTACK_PLAYERS'' | False | Leave this off. |
| ''IGNORE_NAMES'' | empty | Names it should never attack, for example ''["a pack llama"]''. |
| ''BANDAGE_BELOW'' | 60 | Hit point percent that triggers a bandage (0 = never). |
| ''FLEE_BELOW'' | 0 | Hit point percent where it stops fighting and just heals (0 = off). |
