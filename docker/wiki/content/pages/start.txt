====== Shattered Legacy ======

{{:uo:media:shattered_legacy_logo.png?nolink&520|Shattered Legacy crest}}

**One life. Every skill. As many loops as it takes.**

Shattered Legacy is a private Ultima Online shard run for family and friends. This wiki is the player's guide to it. Pages link to each other generously, so you can start with one question and follow it into the world, the systems, the professions and the lore.

===== Server at a glance =====

  * Address: ''shatteredlegacyuo.com'', port ''2593''
  * Client: TazUO, included in the player package from [[https://shatteredlegacyuo.com|shatteredlegacyuo.com]]
  * Accounts: your first login creates yours

The shard's technical documentation lives in its Git repository. This wiki is for what players need: guides, maps, screenshots and the world's flavor.
