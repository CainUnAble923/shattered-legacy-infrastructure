====== Mining ======

Mining feeds Blacksmithy, Tinkering and much of the economy. It works as on OSI, with a longer ladder of metals and the [[:uo:guilds:miners_compact|Miners' Compact]] on top, both unique to Shattered Legacy.

===== Start here =====

^ Item ^ Value ^
| Trainer | Jacob Waltz |
| Area | New Haven, by the mine camp |
| Coordinates | around ''3522, 2531'' |
| Guild | [[:uo:guilds:miners_compact|Miners' Compact]], at the mine camp in New Haven's south mountains |

===== First goals =====

  - Find the trainer, and join the Miners' Compact for a free ore satchel, logbook and Jacob's Pickaxe.
  - Use a pickaxe or shovel on a mountain or cave tile.
  - Smelt the ore at a forge.
  - Carry on into [[blacksmithy|Blacksmithy]].

===== The ores =====

The stock ores (OSI), by base Mining needed to dig them:

^ Ore ^ Mining ^
| Iron | any |
| Dull Copper | 65.0 |
| Shadow Iron | 70.0 |
| Copper | 75.0 |
| Bronze | 80.0 |
| Gold | 85.0 |
| Agapite | 90.0 |
| Verite | 95.0 |
| Valorite | 99.0 |

**Shard change:** eight **extended metals** go past Valorite. The same skill is needed to dig them and to smelt them, and smelting is still a skill roll that can fail.

^ Metal ^ Mining ^
| Platinum | 112.5 |
| Toxic | 125.0 |
| Blaze | 137.5 |
| Frost | 150.0 |
| Obsidian | 162.5 |
| Mythril | 175.0 |
| Adamantium | 187.5 |
| Celestial | 200.0 |

An extended metal must be **reported** to the Miners' Compact before you can keep it; until then its ore comes out as iron. See [[:uo:guilds:miners_compact|Miners' Compact]]. What each metal does in crafting is on [[:uo:professions:materials|Metals and woods]].

A vein keeps its metal after it regrows and after a restart, though a dig can still turn up plain iron now and then.

===== Blackrock =====

At **100 Mining**, about **one dig in a thousand** turns up a small piece of **blackrock**.

===== Stone =====

Mining granite is OSI's: read **Mining For Quality Stone** (sold by Stone Crafters; you need **100.0 base Mining** to read it), then mine stone instead of ore.

**Planned, not in the game yet:** the Compact's Stone Contracts will show only once you have learned stone mining, and the Compact will sell its own copy of the book.

===== Getting higher =====

  * **Climbing boots** from the Compact let you hop onto mountain tiles you cannot walk to. See [[climbing_boots|Climbing boots]].
  * The Compact's **ore satchels** cut ore weight and, from the second tier, smelt at a forge for you.

**Planned, not in the game yet:** the Compact's ore work orders become **mining quotas**: accept, mine the amount, claim. One quota per metal.
