====== Climbing Boots ======

A great deal of Britannia's ore sits in mountainsides that no one can stand close enough to mine. **Climbing boots** from the Miners' Compact let a miner hop up onto a mountain tile and mine it. They are unique to Shattered Legacy: OSI has no climbing.

===== The three boots =====

The **Miners' Compact Liaison** sells them to Compact members. Each upgrade trades in the boots before it.

^ Boots ^ Hop range ^ Compact rank ^ Mining ^ Ore blocks mined ^ Vouchers ^ Gold ^ Ingots ^
| Surveyor's Climbing Boots | 4 tiles | Surveyor | 120 | 100 | 75 | 20,000 | 100 Platinum |
| Delver's Climbing Boots | 8 tiles | Master Delver | 150 | 300 | 150 | 50,000 | 150 Frost |
| Deepwarden's Climbing Boots | 12 tiles | Deepwarden | 180 | 600 | 250 | 100,000 | 200 Mythril |

These are the starting prices; the Liaison shows the current ones.

  * Delver's and Deepwarden's boots cost **half the stamina** per hop.
  * Deepwarden's boots **remember your last ledge**: from the ground within 30 tiles of it, target yourself to hop straight back up.
  * The boots are **blessed** and **bound to your account**. If you lose them, the Liaison replaces them.

**Ore blocks mined** is counted by your **Prospector's Logbook**, which now also remembers when you first mined each block and what ore it gave.

===== Hopping =====

  - Wear the boots and use them.
  - Target a mountain tile (or walkable ground) within your boots' range.
  - Each hop costs stamina (5, plus more the higher you climb) and there is a short pause between hops.

You cannot hop into a town, a dungeon or a house, or anywhere you could not teleport to.

===== On the mountain =====

While you stand on a climbed mountain tile you can only **mine, prospect and hop**:

  * no fighting and no harmful spells,
  * no taming,
  * no Mark,
  * no house placement.

Monsters' spells **can still reach you**. Your pets wait at the foot of the climb and fall in behind you again when you hop down.
