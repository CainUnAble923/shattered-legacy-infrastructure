====== Blacksmithy ======

Blacksmithy turns mining into useful gear and an early economy loop. It works as on OSI, with the shard changes below.

===== Good first loop =====

  - Mine ore using [[mining|Mining]].
  - Smelt ore into ingots at a forge.
  - Craft basic tools or armor.
  - Sell extras or equip upgrades.

Join the [[:uo:guilds:society_of_smiths|Society of Smiths]] early: it is free, it gives you a guild book, a salvage bag and a Hammer of Hephaestus, and its bulk orders and commissions pay as you train.

===== What is different here =====

  * **Recipes are learned.** Designs that need more than 30 skill are hidden until your account reads the pattern book for their band. See [[recipes|Recipes and pattern books]].
  * **Metals past Valorite.** Platinum through Celestial, up to 200 skill. See [[materials|Metals and woods]].
  * **Exceptional gear gets 1.5 times its positive material bonuses.**
  * **Large Smith bulk orders** take crafted items directly, as well as filled small deeds. See [[bulk_orders|Bulk orders]].
  * **The Hammer of Hephaestus**, a Society tool that adds Blacksmithy while held and learns each metal you work.
