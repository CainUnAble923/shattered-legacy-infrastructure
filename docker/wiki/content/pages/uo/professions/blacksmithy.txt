====== Blacksmithy ======

Blacksmithy turns mining into useful gear and an early economy loop.

===== Good First Loop =====

  - Mine ore using [[mining|Mining]].
  - Smelt ore into ingots.
  - Craft basic tools or armor.
  - Sell extras or equip upgrades.
