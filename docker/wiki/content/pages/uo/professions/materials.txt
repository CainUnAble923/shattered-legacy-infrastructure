====== Metals and Woods ======

Shattered Legacy has eight metals past Valorite and eight woods past Frostwood. They are unique to this shard, and they are why crafting skills go to 200: each one needs more skill than the last, and working it teaches your craft up to the next one's requirement.

===== The metals =====

OSI's metals, Iron through Valorite, are unchanged. Above them:

^ Metal ^ Mining and Blacksmithy needed ^ Crafting in it teaches Blacksmithy up to ^
| Platinum | 112.5 | 125 |
| Toxic | 125 | 137.5 |
| Blaze | 137.5 | 150 |
| Frost | 150 | 162.5 |
| Obsidian | 162.5 | 175 |
| Mythril | 175 | 187.5 |
| Adamantium | 187.5 | 200 |
| Celestial | 200 | 200 |

  * **Mining**: below a metal's step its vein gives iron. Ore smelts at the same step, in the forge's usual 25-point window.
  * **Tinkering** works these ingots at the same skill smiths need, and climbs the same way.
  * **Stone**: a Grandmaster stone miner digging Platinum through Celestial turns up that metal's granite for masons.
  * The chance to make an item is unchanged; only how far it teaches you is new.

===== The woods =====

OSI's woods, Oak through Frostwood, are unchanged. Above them, for Carpentry and Bowcraft/Fletching:

^ Wood ^ Carpentry or Fletching needed ^ Teaches up to ^
| Ironwood | 100 | 115 |
| Ghostwood | 115 | 130 |
| Emberbark | 130 | 140 |
| Frostbark | 140 | 155 |
| Shadowbark | 155 | 170 |
| Runewood | 170 | 185 |
| Voidwood | 185 | 200 |
| Starwood | 200 | 200 |

  * **Lumberjacking**: every shard wood needs 100 Lumberjacking to chop, and the rarer the wood the rarer its grove. Each tree keeps the same wood for good, so a grove in your logging book always leads back to that wood.
  * Logs and boards of each wood show their own color and name.
  * Cutting logs into boards teaches nothing; only crafting with the wood does.

===== Past the item's own top =====

Normally a craft stops teaching once your skill passes an item's top. In a shard metal or wood it keeps teaching up to the next material's requirement, and harder items teach faster: a plate tunic in Platinum trains a smith better than a dagger in Platinum. Worn skill bonuses do not cut this short; the material teaches until your own skill reaches its limit.

===== Starter materials =====

Two more materials exist for crafters around 45 skill: **Dawnstone** ore and **Rootwood**, found only in the Rootdeep Burrows, with **Burrowhide** leather and **Gloomsilk** cloth from the Burrows' creatures. They teach like the OSI materials do. The Burrows are still being dug; see [[:uo:drafts:rootdeep_burrows|Rootdeep Burrows]].

===== Tools that know the ladder =====

  * The Miners' Compact's **ore satchels** and **Jacob's pickaxes** are colored by tier, following the ore ladder, and the satchels from the Reinforced one up smelt ore at a forge exactly as the forge does.
  * The Society of Smiths' **Hammer of Hephaestus** gives its Blacksmithy bonus while you hold it and grows **Metal Familiarity** per metal as you craft, up to 1,000 per metal (2,500 on the Reinforced hammer), with an exceptional piece counting 3.
  * The Society's **salvage bag** returns more ingots with higher Mining, but never more than the item cost.
