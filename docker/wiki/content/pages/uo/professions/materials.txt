====== Metals and Woods ======

Shattered Legacy has eight metals past Valorite and eight woods past Frostwood. They are unique to this shard, and they are why crafting skills go to 200: each one needs more skill than the last, and working it teaches your craft up to the next one's requirement.

===== The metals =====

OSI's metals, Iron through Valorite, are unchanged. Above them:

^ Metal ^ Mining and Blacksmithy needed ^ Crafting in it teaches Blacksmithy up to ^
| Platinum | 112.5 | 125 |
| Toxic | 125 | 137.5 |
| Blaze | 137.5 | 150 |
| Frost | 150 | 162.5 |
| Obsidian | 162.5 | 175 |
| Mythril | 175 | 187.5 |
| Adamantium | 187.5 | 200 |
| Celestial | 200 | 200 |

  * **Mining**: below a metal's step its vein gives iron. Ore smelts at the same step, in the forge's usual 25-point window.
  * **Tinkering** works these ingots at the same skill smiths need, and climbs the same way.
  * **Stone**: a Grandmaster stone miner digging Platinum through Celestial turns up that metal's granite for masons.
  * The chance to make an item is unchanged; only how far it teaches you is new.

===== The woods =====

OSI's woods, Oak through Frostwood, are unchanged. Above them, for Carpentry and Bowcraft/Fletching:

^ Wood ^ Carpentry or Fletching needed ^ Teaches up to ^
| Ironwood | 100 | 115 |
| Ghostwood | 115 | 130 |
| Emberbark | 130 | 140 |
| Frostbark | 140 | 155 |
| Shadowbark | 155 | 170 |
| Runewood | 170 | 185 |
| Voidwood | 185 | 200 |
| Starwood | 200 | 200 |

The table above is for **crafting**. Chopping is a separate ladder:

  * **Lumberjacking**: every shard wood needs 100 Lumberjacking to chop, and the rarer the wood the rarer its grove. Each tree keeps the same wood for good, so a grove in your logging book always leads back to that wood.
  * Logs and boards of each wood show their own color and name.
  * Cutting logs into boards teaches nothing; only crafting with the wood does.

===== What the woods give =====

The shard woods add bonuses to **bows, staves, clubs and wooden shields** made from them. A normal item gets the first column; an exceptional one gets both. These woods never add Self Repair. OSI's woods keep OSI's bonuses (bows only).

^ Wood ^ Weapon ^ Exceptional weapon adds ^ Shield ^ Exceptional shield adds ^
| Ironwood | Durability +50 | Damage +14, lower stat requirements 20 | Durability +50 | Physical resist +3, lower stat requirements 20 |
| Ghostwood | Hit chance +6 | Damage +15, defense chance +5 | Defense chance +4 | Energy resist +3, defense chance +3 |
| Emberbark | Half its damage as fire | Damage +16, Hit Fire Area 10 | Fire resist +4 | Fire resist +3, reflect physical 5 |
| Frostbark | 60 percent of its damage as cold | Damage +17, Hit Lower Attack 10 | Cold resist +4 | Cold resist +3, reflect physical 5 |
| Shadowbark | Swing speed +10 | Damage +18, hit chance +6 | Defense chance +5 | Poison resist +3, defense chance +3 |
| Runewood | Half its damage as energy, mana regeneration +2 | Damage +20, mana leech 15 | Energy resist +4, mana regeneration +1 | Spell channeling |
| Voidwood | Life leech 15 | Damage +22, stamina leech 15 | All resists +1 | All resists +1, hit point regeneration +2 |
| Starwood | Luck +100 | Damage +25, hit chance +8, one random Heartwood-style extra | Luck +100 | All resists +2, defense chance +5 |

Rootwood, the starter wood, adds no bonus.

===== Exceptional items and material bonuses =====

**Shard change.** An exceptional item gets **one and a half times its material's positive bonuses**, rounded half up, for every metal, leather and scale, OSI's included: a +5 becomes +8. Penalties stay the same, runic properties are not multiplied, and the normal caps still apply. OSI's woods get the multiplier on their bow bonuses. The shard woods do **not**: their exceptional column above is used instead. Cloth has no material bonus.

===== Past the item's own top =====

Normally a craft stops teaching once your skill passes an item's top. In a shard metal or wood it keeps teaching up to the next material's requirement, and harder items teach faster: a plate tunic in Platinum trains a smith better than a dagger in Platinum. Worn skill bonuses do not cut this short; the material teaches until your own skill reaches its limit.

===== Starter materials =====

Two more materials exist for crafters around 45 skill: **Dawnstone** ore and **Rootwood**, found only in the Rootdeep Burrows, with **Burrowhide** leather and **Gloomsilk** cloth from the Burrows' creatures. They teach like the OSI materials do. The Burrows are not open yet; see [[:uo:drafts:rootdeep_burrows|Rootdeep Burrows]].

===== Tools that know the ladder =====

  * The Miners' Compact's **ore satchels** and **Jacob's pickaxes** are colored by tier, following the ore ladder, and the satchels from the Reinforced one up smelt ore at a forge exactly as the forge does.
  * The Society of Smiths' **Hammer of Hephaestus** gives its Blacksmithy bonus while you hold it and grows **Metal Familiarity** per metal as you craft, up to 1,000 per metal (2,500 on the Reinforced hammer), with an exceptional piece counting 3.
  * The Society's **salvage bag** returns more ingots with higher Mining, but never more than the item cost.
