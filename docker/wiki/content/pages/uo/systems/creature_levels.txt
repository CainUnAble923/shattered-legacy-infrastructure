====== Creature Levels and Appraisal ======

Every creature and NPC on Shattered Legacy has a **level**, and you can see at a glance how tough it is next to you. The **Item Identification** skill is now called **Appraisal** and works on creatures as well as items. Both are unique to Shattered Legacy: OSI shows no creature levels.

===== The colored word =====

Click a creature (or show all names) and a colored word appears under its name:

^ Color ^ Word ^ Its level against yours ^
| grey | trivial | 20 or more below you |
| green | easy | 10 to 19 below |
| blue | fair | 3 to 9 below |
| yellow | even match | within 2 either way |
| orange | tough | 3 to 9 above |
| red | dangerous | 10 to 19 above |
| dark red | deadly | 20 or more above |

The word is there so the color is never mistaken for a name's notoriety color (blue, grey, red), which is unchanged.

A creature's level runs from 1 to 200 and follows how hard it is for a bard to work on (OSI's Bard difficulty). Champions are 200, peerless bosses 190. Shopkeepers and other NPCs that cannot be harmed are level 1 and always grey.

Until character levels arrive, **your level is your best combat skill**.

===== Appraisal =====

Appraisal is the old Item Identification. On items it works as it always did. On creatures:

^ Appraisal ^ What you learn ^
| any | a feel for the creature (anyone can try) |
| 30 | its level |
| 60 | its hits, stats and resistances |
| 90 | a stat card: its damage, its special abilities, and whether it is a paragon or a boss |

Appraisal trains past 100 on creatures near your skill. The skill window calls it "Appraisal" once you update your game through the launcher.
