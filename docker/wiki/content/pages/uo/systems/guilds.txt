====== Guilds ======

Shattered Legacy's guilds are not player guilds: they are **eighteen trade and combat guilds** with halls in New Haven, each teaching a set of skills, selling its own goods for its own currency, and ranking you by the work you do for it. They are the starter path for a new character and the backbone of the [[league|League]]. The guild system is unique to this shard.

The "show" column is the **Apprentice task**, not a gate: joining is free and takes one click at the guildmaster. Meeting the task later pays your first standing and currency and raises you to Apprentice.

===== Joining and the directory =====

  - Talk to a guildmaster in New Haven. The guild's hall page opens.
  - Press **Join**. You are an Initiate, and you get the guild's starter tools (the Order of the Alembic hands you bottles, a mortar and pestle and a robe, for example).
  - Type ''[guild'' any time to open the **Guild Directory**: every guild, your standing and rank in each, and a **Show me the way** button that points you to the nearest guildmaster and names the town.

You can join as many guilds as you like. Membership, standing and currency are per character.

===== Standing, currency and rank =====

Every guild keeps two numbers for you:

  * **Standing**, earned by the guild's work: its bulk orders, work orders and commissions, [[requisitions|requisitions]], and for the Custodians your lifetime Clean Up points. Standing only rises.
  * **Currency**, the guild's own scrip, spent in its shop: Smithing Seals, Mining Vouchers, Timber Tokens, Trail Marks, Essence Shards, and so on.

Rank follows standing:

^ Rank ^ Standing ^
| Initiate | 0 |
| Apprentice | 1,000 |
| Journeyman | 5,000 |
| Master | 15,000 |
| Grandmaster | 50,000 |
| Legendary | 100,000 |

The Rangers' League uses its own rank names (Wanderer, Scout, Outrider, Trailblazer, Beastmaster, Warden of the Wild, Legendary Outrider) on the same idea.

A **crafting guild** also asks for its craft skill: 30 for Apprentice, then 50, 70, 90 and 100 for Legendary. A rank you have reached is never lost, even when a loop resets your skills. Rising in a crafting guild hands you that rank's pattern book (see [[:uo:professions:recipes|Recipes and pattern books]]).

===== What the guilds sell =====

  * **Pattern books and recipe scrolls** for the guild's craft, one band ahead of your rank.
  * **Bulk orders** for members, with better pay than the town vendors give (see [[:uo:professions:bulk_orders|Bulk orders]]).
  * **Pack mule modules**: each guild's hall sells the schematics for its module (see [[pack_mules|Pack mules]]).
  * Guild tools and upgrades, such as the Miners' Compact's ore satchels and Jacob's pickaxes, or the Smiths' Hammer of Hephaestus.

Guild costs and turn-ins take materials from your **bank** as well as your backpack, pack first.
