====== Pet Training ======

Pet training is OSI's **Time of Legends** animal training, ported as it is: your pet earns training points in battle and spends them on stats, resistances, skills and special abilities. Anything you read on UOGuide or Stratics about animal training applies here. The few places Shattered Legacy differs are listed at the end.

===== Starting training =====

  - Use **Animal Lore** on a pet you control.
  - Press **Begin Animal Training** on the lore page.
  - From then on its battles against wild creatures fill a training bar. The pet says how much it learned from each kill, and the first hour of fighting each day counts double.
  - When the bar is full, **Pet Training Options** opens the animal training menu.

Animal trainers in town mention the new taming techniques when you walk up.

===== What you can train =====

Spend the pet's training points on strength, dexterity, intelligence, hit points, stamina, mana, regeneration, base damage, the five resistances, higher skill caps (with the matching power scroll in your pack), magical schools, special abilities, special moves and area effects. Each shows its cost and its limits, and a planning mode lets you line choices up before spending. A real-time tracker shows every pet in training.

The Animal Lore page has new pages too: regeneration, every skill with its cap, the pet's control slots (current and maximum) and the taming skill it asks for, its abilities, and the advancements it has trained.

===== Control slots =====

Tameable creatures use the Time of Legends control slots. Every pet starts at the minimum for its kind and rises one slot per training level up to its maximum, asking for more Animal Taming as it goes. A dragon is 4 slots (5 fully trained), a white wyrm 4, a nightmare 3, a bear 1 (3 fully trained).

A pet that has been trained needs the Animal Taming its training raised it to, not its wild requirement, to be tamed again. A trained pet's special moves (Armor Ignore, Bleed Attack, Dismount and the rest) are the ones it uses in combat, and pets in training gain Focus.

===== Where Shattered Legacy differs =====

  * **Stable slots** keep rising past 120: 14 slots at Animal Taming, Animal Lore and Veterinary 120, up to **23** at 200 in all three.
  * **Angering a beast** (dragons, nightmares, the Dread Warhorse and others that can be angered) gets less likely as your Taming rises above the creature's minimum: 95% at the minimum, about 77% twelve points above it, about 32% at forty-two, and 15% from about fifty-four points above.
  * **Animal Lore past 120** gains on creatures that are hard to tame, the same ones Animal Taming trains on. See [[skill_gain|Skill gain]].
  * **Veterinary and Animal Lore** at 200 heal a pet about 72 with a bandage (about 57 at 120).
  * **A tamed creature that poisons** does it the way trained pets do: its Poisoning skill decides how often and how strongly, up to Lethal at 100.
  * The **iron beetle** mines ore for its owner, as on OSI.
  * **Seasoned owners teach faster** (below).

===== Seasoned owners teach faster =====

**Shard change.** On OSI a pet's skills learn at the same speed whoever owns it. Here an experienced owner makes a pet's skills gain more often. The Animal Lore skill pages show it as **Learns x...**.

The bonus comes from your Animal Taming and Animal Lore above 100 (each counted up to 200, worn bonuses included) and your rank in the [[:uo:guilds:rangers_league|Rangers' League]] (the Outriders) while you are a member.

^ Owner ^ Learns ^
| Taming and Lore at 100, no Rangers' rank | x1.00 |
| Taming and Lore at 120, no rank | x1.16 |
| Taming and Lore at 200, no rank | x1.80 |
| Taming and Lore at 200, Legendary Outrider | x2.40 |

It makes each skill gain more likely. It does not make gains bigger, set a learning time, or speed up stat gains. It covers every pet skill, including Focus while the pet is in training. A loop bonus on top, up to x3, is planned but not working yet.

===== More creatures to tame =====

The port brought the blood fox, cold drake, crimson and platinum drakes, dragon wolf, frost dragon, frost mite, lion, ossein ram, saber-toothed tiger, triceratops, stygian drake, iron beetle, and the wild, white and black tigers, with their natural abilities (dragon breath, grasping claw, life leech, auras, howls). Fully tamed pets keep those abilities.
