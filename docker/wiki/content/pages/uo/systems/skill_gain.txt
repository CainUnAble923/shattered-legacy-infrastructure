====== Skill Gain ======

Skill gain is intentionally faster than stock ModernUO so players can explore builds without a long grind.

===== Current ClusterF Settings =====

^ Setting ^ Value ^
| Applies to | Players |
| Chance attempts | ''3'' |
| Amount multiplier | ''5'' |

===== What This Means =====

Players should see skills move noticeably faster during normal play. This supports experimentation across crafting, combat, gathering, and support skills.
