====== Loot Boxes ======

A loot box is a reward you get the first time you do something notable: earn an [[achievements|achievement]], slay a boss, or reach a [[league|League]] rank. What is inside is decided the moment you earn it, so holding on to a box changes nothing. Loot boxes are unique to Shattered Legacy.

===== How you get one =====

^ Source ^ When ^ Tier ^
| An achievement | The first time your account earns it | By the achievement's AP: 1 to 6 |
| A boss | Your first kill of each boss, for everyone with looting rights | 4 as a rule; 5 for a champion or a peerless boss; 6 for the Harrower |
| A League rank | Each rank the first time you reach it | 1 at Iron, rising to 6 at Adamantium and Celestial |

Graduating from the New Haven tutorial also hands you your first box. The [[league_shop|League Shop]] sells the **System's Loot Boxes** (Bronze, Silver and Gold) for Renown; they hold League goods only, no gold or Renown. Some secret [[achievements|achievements]] pay a **Dented Box**: a small box with one guaranteed, entirely useless keepsake inside, bound to you. Doing the same deed again on a later loop gives no second box, only a line from the System and a count on your achievements page.

===== Tiers and themes =====

Boxes run from **tier 1 to tier 6**. A higher tier holds more and better things.

^ Tier ^ Items inside ^ Gold ^ Renown ^
| 1 | 2 | 250 | none |
| 2 | 3 | 750 | 25 |
| 3 | 3 | 2,000 | 50 |
| 4 | 4 | 5,000 | 100 |
| 5 | 5 | 12,000 | 200 |
| 6 | 6 | 30,000 | 400 |

Every box has a slot for its theme and a slot for gold (and Renown from tier 2). The rest is drawn from pools of consumables, resources, magic gear, recipe scrolls, cosmetics and rare things; low tiers lean toward consumables and resources, high tiers toward gear, cosmetics and rarities.

A box is named for its theme and tier. The gathering and crafting themes take their names from the material ladders: a **Miner's** box is a Dull Copper, Bronze, Verite, Valorite, Mythril or Celestial Box; a **Woodsman's** box an Oak, Yew, Heartwood, Frostwood, Runewood or Celestial Box; a **Hunter's** box a Leather, Spined, Horned, Barbed or Celestial Box. Artisan's, Explorer's, Tamer's, Scholar's, Boss, Loop and League boxes run **Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary, Celestial**.

Box gear is always weaker than exceptional crafted gear of the same tier.

===== Opening a box =====

  * Open it in a **guarded town**, or in a **house** where you are the owner, a co-owner or a friend. Anywhere else the System refuses.
  * The box is **bound to your account**. So are the cosmetics inside it. Everything else inside can be traded.
  * The box vanishes when it is empty. A box opened and left with things in it (a full bank, say) is cleared away after 7 days.

===== Flying loot =====

Opening a box sends the best thing inside **flying out of it**: a sword, a bow, a staff, a piece of armor, a shield, a ring, a gem, a potion or a bit of clothing arcs up out of your pack and lands at your feet, just before the fanfare. A box of tier 4 and up shows it to everyone around you; a smaller one to you alone. It is only a show: what the box gives you is the same either way, and boxes holding only gold, resources or the like skip it. Update your game through the launcher to see it.

===== Fanfare =====

Earning a box plays a fanfare: fireworks and a line from the System. Tier 4 and above make a bigger show; a first boss kill is announced to the shard. ''[FanfareMute'' silences your own fanfare and your flying loot; shard-wide announcements always show.
