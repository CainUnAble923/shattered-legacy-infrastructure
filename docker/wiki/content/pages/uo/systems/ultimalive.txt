====== UltimaLive ======

UltimaLive is being tracked as a possible live-map extension for Shattered Legacy.

===== Current ClusterF Status =====

The server-side compatibility layer exists, but UltimaLive is disabled until client support and asset behavior are fully verified.

===== Decision Points =====

  * Confirm ClassicUO compatibility.
  * Confirm client files and patching workflow.
  * Decide whether live map edits are needed for the shard.
  * Document rollback steps before enabling.
