====== The League Register ======

The League Register is a book that holds your [[guilds|guilds]] in one place: their ranks, standing and currency, and, as your [[league|League]] rank rises, things you can do from anywhere. It also sells **League goods** for Renown. It is unique to Shattered Legacy.

===== Getting one =====

From **Dull Copper Citizen**, the League Registrar in New Haven gives you a League Register, free. It is blessed and belongs to that character. If you lose it, she issues a new one, and the old copy stops working.

===== Binding guilds =====

Bind a guild you belong to into the Register. A guild that has its own book (the Smith Guild Book, the Dispatch Ledger and the like) gives its book up to the Register; you can take it back out, free, at any time.

How many guilds it holds depends on your League rank:

^ League rank ^ Guilds the Register holds ^
| Dull Copper | 2 |
| Bronze | 4 |
| Verite | 6 |
| Blaze | 9 |
| Mythril | 12 |
| Celestial | all 18 |

If you leave a guild, the Register shows it grayed out once and then drops it, which frees its slot.

===== The pages =====

  * **Your bound guilds.**
  * **Guild Ledger**: every guild's rank, standing and currency at a glance.
  * **League actions**, opening with your rank:
    * **Bronze**: open any bound guild's contracts from anywhere.
    * **Verite**: turn in every ready order of your bound guilds at once.
    * **Blaze**: repair the gear you are wearing, free. It is for your own worn gear only.
  * **Bank**: counts the gold and materials in your bank.
  * **League goods** (below).

===== League goods =====

Paid in Renown, with a little gold. Like the [[league_shop|League Shop]], this spends your Renown balance only, never your Lifetime Renown, so it never lowers your League rank. These are the starting prices; the Register shows the current ones.

^ Item ^ League rank ^ Renown ^ Gold ^ What it does ^
| Combined Logbook | Dull Copper | 500 | 50 | Your Prospector's and Forester's logbooks in one |
| League Field Satchel | Bronze | 5,000 | 500 | Holds your ore, lumber, hunter's and essence satchels and counts as one item. Each satchel you put in costs a small fee (50) in that satchel's guild currency |
| Bigger backpack | Bronze | 2,000 | 200 | Holds 150 items |
| Bigger backpack | Verite | 6,000 | 600 | Holds 175 items |
| Bigger backpack | Blaze | 15,000 | 1,500 | Holds 200 items |
| Bigger backpack | Mythril | 30,000 | 3,000 | Holds 250 items |

A bigger backpack raises the **item count** only. The weight you can carry does not change.

===== Repair items (for smiths) =====

Blacksmiths with **Arms Lore** can make three repair items. Find them under **Repair Items** in the smithing menu.

^ Item ^ Blacksmithy ^ Arms Lore ^ Materials ^ What it does ^
| Field repair deed | 60 | 90 | 5 iron ingots | Repairs one item, once |
| Field repair kit | 80 | 110 | 30 iron ingots | Repairs one item per charge, 10 charges; works on tools that wear, too |
| Remote repair deed | 90 | 120 | 20 iron ingots, 1 star sapphire | Repairs everything you wear, carry and bank, once |

A repair from one of these brings the item back to full durability without lowering its maximum.
