====== Achievements ======

Achievements are Shattered Legacy's record of what you have done: first kills, skills reached, places visited, metals mined, guilds joined. Each one pays **Achievement Points** (AP) and **Renown**, and the first time your account earns one you get a [[loot_boxes|loot box]]. The system is unique to this shard.

===== Opening the page =====

  * Type ''[achievements'' in game, or
  * press the **Achievements** button on your paperdoll (where Help used to be) or on the top bar. The button comes with the Shattered Legacy client in the player package.

The page lists every achievement by category, with what it needs, your progress toward it, and what earned it once you have it. Each category has a heading with its own icon (crossed swords for Combat, an open book for Skills, and so on); update your game through the launcher to see the icons, or the headings show the names alone.

The window also opens the [[league_shop|League Shop]] and the **Titles** list.

===== Categories =====

^ Category ^ What it covers ^
| Combat | First kill, kill counts, named foes |
| Skills | Skill milestones, including the shard's 200 cap |
| Exploration | Logging in, visiting places such as Old Haven |
| Mining | Ore and the shard's own metals |
| Crafting | Crafted items and exceptional work |
| Legacy | Longer-term goals |
| Discovery | Finding things for the first time |
| Collection | Collecting sets of things |
| League | Joining and rising in the [[league|League]] |
| Mischief | Secret joke achievements. You will not see this category until you earn one (below) |

Many achievements are **secret**: they do not show in the locked list, and appear with a [Secret] tag once earned. Six old skill achievements that asked for skill above 200 are **retired**: nobody can earn them now, but anyone who has one keeps it and its points, under a Retired heading.

===== Mischief: the System is watching =====

The **Mischief** category is all secret, and it is where the System keeps its notes on you. It has no tab of its own: earned ones show under **All**, with a grinning imp for an icon. There are three kinds:

  * **Britannia's sillier moments**: the old jokes the world has always had. Your parents probably warned you about at least one of them.
  * **Noted by the System**: the shard's favorite shortcuts and odd habits, in fights, on the road, at the forge, at home and around loot boxes.
  * **A few things about your feet.** Two of these open [[accessories|toe ring slots]].

We won't say what any of them are: you find out when you do them. Each is earned once per account and comes with a few words from the System. Some come with a **Dented Box**: a small [[loot_boxes|loot box]] holding one guaranteed, entirely useless keepsake, bound to you. Three come with a title, which shows in your Titles list only once you hold it. A few cannot be earned yet, because what they notice is not on the shard yet.

===== What an achievement pays =====

  * **AP**, from 5 for a first kill to 150 for the biggest skill goals. 100 AP puts you "officially on the board".
  * **Renown**, which counts toward your [[league|League rank]] and which you can spend in the [[league_shop|League Shop]] without losing rank.
  * AP opens rows in the [[league_shop|League Shop]] and free **League titles** at 100, 250, 500, 1,000 and 1,750 AP.
  * A **loot box** the first time your account earns it. The box's tier follows the achievement's AP: up to 10 AP is tier 1, up to 25 tier 2, up to 50 tier 3, up to 100 tier 4, up to 250 tier 5, and above that tier 6.
  * Some achievements also hand you an item.

Achievements are shared by your whole account. A second character on the account does not earn the same one again, and does not get a second box for it. The page shows "Earned on loops" with a count when a character earns one again on a later loop.

===== Fanfare =====

Earning an achievement plays a fanfare: fireworks and a line from the System, which has opinions. Bigger deeds make a bigger show, and a few are announced to the whole shard. ''[FanfareMute'' turns your own fanfare off; shard-wide announcements still show.
