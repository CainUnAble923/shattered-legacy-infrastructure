====== Achievements ======

Achievements are Shattered Legacy's record of what you have done: first kills, skills reached, places visited, metals mined, guilds joined. Each one pays **Achievement Points** (AP), most also pay **Renown**, and the first time your account earns one you get a [[loot_boxes|loot box]]. The system is unique to this shard.

===== Opening your records =====

  * Type ''[achievements'' in game,
  * press the **Achievements** button on your paperdoll (where Help used to be) or on the top bar (it comes with the Shattered Legacy client in the player package), or
  * ask Elara Voss, the [[league|League Registrar]] in New Haven.

The window shows your AP and Renown at the top. Earned entries say what you did and what they paid. Unfinished ones show what they need, with prerequisites and a progress count where one applies. Each category has a heading with its own icon (crossed swords for Combat, an open book for Skills, and so on); update your game through the launcher to see the icons, or the headings show the names alone.

The window also opens the [[league_shop|League Shop]] and the **Titles** list. The **Quests** tab is a separate checklist for the New Haven trainer quests.

===== Categories =====

Use **All** to see every category. Collection, League and Mischief show there without tabs of their own.

^ Category ^ What it covers ^
| Combat | Creature kills and combat milestones |
| Skills | Skill milestones, and developing several skills |
| Exploration | Arriving in Britannia and visiting its facets |
| Mining | Colored ore, ore discoveries and mining work |
| Crafting | Making items, exceptional work and smelting |
| Legacy | The New Haven trainer quests and work in Old Haven |
| Discovery | Survey reports and treasure map finds |
| Collection | Collecting ore types and reaching bank gold milestones |
| League | Registering, League activities and achievement milestones |
| Mischief | Secret achievements found through play (below) |

Many achievements are **secret**: they stay out of the unfinished list until you earn one, then show with a [Secret] tag. Secret ones hide in other categories too, not only in Mischief.

Older characters may see a **Retired** heading: a few old skill achievements that nobody can earn now. Anyone who has one keeps it and its points.

===== Mischief: the System is watching =====

The **Mischief** category is all secret, and it is where the System keeps its notes on you. Earned ones show under **All**, with a grinning imp for an icon. It holds three kinds:

  * **Britannia's sillier moments**: the old jokes the world has always had.
  * **Noted by the System**: the shard's favorite shortcuts and odd habits, in fights, on the road, at the forge, at home and around loot boxes.
  * **A few things about your feet.** Some of these open [[accessories|toe ring slots]].

We won't say what any of them are: you find out when you do them. Each is earned once per account and comes with a few words from the System. Some pay a **Dented Box**: a small [[loot_boxes|loot box]] holding one guaranteed, entirely useless keepsake, bound to you. Some come with a title, which shows in your Titles list only once you hold it. A few cannot be earned yet, because what they notice is not on the shard yet.

===== What an achievement pays =====

**AP opens, Renown buys.**

^ Number ^ What it is ^
| Achievement Points (AP) | Your permanent score. It opens rows in the [[league_shop|League Shop]] and free titles. It is never spent. |
| Renown | Your spendable League balance. Achievements, earned loot boxes and [[requisitions|requisitions]] add to it. Some achievements pay no Renown. |
| Lifetime Renown | The Renown you have earned through play, which your [[league|League rank]] is measured by. Spending Renown never lowers it. |

  * 100 AP puts you "officially on the board".
  * A **loot box** the first time your account earns an achievement. Its tier follows that achievement's AP: up to 10 AP is tier 1, up to 25 tier 2, up to 50 tier 3, up to 100 tier 4, up to 250 tier 5, and above that tier 6.
  * Some achievements also hand you an item.

Achievements belong to your **account**: your characters share the earned list, the AP and the Renown. A second character does not earn the same one again, and does not get a second box for it.

===== Titles =====

Open **Titles** from your achievement records, the [[league_shop|League Shop]] or the Registrar, and pick one to show after your character's name, or **No League title** to clear it. Titles you own belong to the account; each character picks its own.

These open free as your AP grows, and stay yours:

^ AP ^ Title ^
| 100 | the Noted |
| 250 | the Accomplished |
| 500 | the Distinguished |
| 1,000 | the Renowned |
| 1,750 | the Illustrious |

The League Shop also sells titles for Renown: //the Registered Adventurer//, //Veteran of the League// and //the System's Favorite//. Secret titles appear in your list only once you hold them.

===== Fanfare =====

Earning an achievement plays a fanfare: fireworks, a sound and a line from the System, which has opinions. Bigger deeds make a bigger show, and a few are announced to the whole shard. ''[FanfareMute'' turns your own fanfare off; shard-wide announcements still show. See [[loot_boxes|Loot boxes]] for who sees what.
