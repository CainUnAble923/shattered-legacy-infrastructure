====== New Player Guide ======

Welcome to Shattered Legacy. This page should answer the first questions a new player has after logging in.

===== First Choices =====

  * If you want to gather and craft, start with [[:uo:professions:mining|Mining]] and [[:uo:professions:blacksmithy|Blacksmithy]], and read [[:uo:professions:recipes|Recipes]] to see how crafting is learned here.
  * If you want smoother combat training, read [[:uo:professions:focus|Focus]] and [[:uo:systems:skill_gain|Skill gain]].
  * If you want quests and NPC direction, start in [[:uo:world:new_haven|New Haven]].
  * Whatever you pick, join a [[:uo:systems:guilds|guild]] and register with the [[:uo:systems:league|League]] early. They are free.

===== Quick Route =====

  - Connect using [[:uo:connection|Connection guide]].
  - Create your character. You arrive on the New Haven plaza.
  - Find the nearest trainer using [[:uo:world:trainers|Trainer index]].
  - Pick up early quests from [[:uo:world:quests|Quest givers]].
  - Follow [[:uo:guides:first_hour|First hour route]].
  - Type ''[guild'' to join a guild, and visit the League Registrar.

===== Server Differences =====

  * Every skill caps at 200, and gains come about twice as often as on a stock server.
  * Individual stats can reach 500.
  * New accounts are Young for two weeks of play.
  * Your bank holds 1,000 items.
  * Crafting is learned from pattern books and recipe scrolls.
  * Achievements and League promotions pay loot boxes.

See [[:uo:systems:start|Server systems]] for the details, the [[:uo:newplayer:faq|New player FAQ]] for quick answers, and the [[:uo:newplayer:glossary|Glossary]] for words you do not know.
