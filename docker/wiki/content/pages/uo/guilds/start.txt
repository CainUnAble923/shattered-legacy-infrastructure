====== The Guilds ======

Shattered Legacy has eighteen trade and combat guilds of its own. Start with the [[:uo:systems:guilds|guilds overview]]: how to join, standing, currency, ranks, and which guild suits you. The pages below cover single guilds in more depth.

===== Crafting and enchanting =====

  * [[society_of_smiths|Society of Smiths]]: Blacksmithy, bulk orders, commissions, the Hammer of Hephaestus
  * [[artificers_order|Artificers' Order]]: Imbuing, formula books, unraveling, enchanting work orders
  * [[order_of_the_alembic|Order of the Alembic]]: Alchemy, Taste Identification, Glassblowing

===== Gathering and the wilds =====

  * [[miners_compact|Miners' Compact]]: Mining, ore satchels, the Prospector's Logbook, climbing boots
  * [[foresters_union|Foresters' Union]]: Lumberjacking, the Forester's Logbook, lumber satchels
  * [[rangers_league|Rangers' League]]: the Outriders, animal work, Herding and the crook

===== Service and the shadows =====

  * [[healers_covenant|Healers' Covenant]]: Healing and Anatomy
  * [[custodians|The Custodians]]: Clean Up Britannia and its store
  * [[thieves_den|Thieves' Den]]: monster stealing, Den scrip, trap perks

The other guilds (Arcane Society, Tailors' Circle, Tinkers' Union, Bards' Consortium, Warriors' Brotherhood, Twin Paths Dojo, Keepers of the Last Door, Maritime Brotherhood and Merchants' Exchange) are covered on the [[:uo:systems:guilds|overview]] for now.
