====== Treasures of Sorcerer's Dungeon ======

Every Halloween, **October 24 to November 2**, Sorcerer's Dungeon in **Ilshenar** fills with the undead and nine holiday bosses. It is OSI's Treasures of Sorcerer's Dungeon, ported as OSI has it, on our Halloween dates. It starts and ends by itself.

===== How it works =====

  * The event works through **nine rooms of the dungeon, one at a time**. Zombies, skeletons, gargoyles, liches and lich lords keep coming in the current room.
  * When **80** of them have died in that room, the room's **boss** appears.
  * When the boss is dead, the event moves on to the next room after a minute or three.

The bosses, in order: the nightmare fairy, the jack in the box, the headless elf, the abominable snowman, the twisted holiday tree, the rabid reindeer, the garish gingerman, the stocking serpent, and finally **Jack the Pumpkin King**. Then it starts again from the first room.

===== Artifacts of Enchanted Origin =====

While the event runs, slaying monsters in Sorcerer's Dungeon now and then puts a piece of equipment **"of Enchanted Origin"** in your backpack: a weapon, armor, shield, jewel or hat with random properties, in its own hue. You can wear it, or trade it in.

The **Researcher** stands by the dungeon entrance, beside the event's pumpkins and flaming scarecrows. He takes each artifact of Enchanted Origin for **one point**, and sells rewards for points:

^ Reward ^ Points ^
| Jack's Bag of Tricks | 75 |
| Serpent's Jawbone | 50 |
| Sterling Silver Ring | 50 |
| Talons of Escaping | 50 |
| Boots of Escaping | 50 |
| Jack in the Box | 20 |
| Shackled Heart of the Pumpkin King | 20 |

**Shard change:** OSI's three title deeds (Hero of the Unloved, Savior of the Demented, Slayer of the Pumpkin King) are not offered, because the shard has no reward title menu yet.

After the event ends, the Researcher stays, so you can still spend your points.
