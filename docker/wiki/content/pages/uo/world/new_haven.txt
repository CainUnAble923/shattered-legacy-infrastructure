====== New Haven ======

{{:uo:media:new_haven_route.svg?nolink&700|New Haven route diagram}}

New Haven is the starting town: the tutorial, the guild halls, the League's field office and the trainers are all here.

===== Who to find =====

^ Who ^ What for ^ Where ^
| Rook Hallowell, League recruiter | Runs the new player tutorial | The plaza, two tiles from where you arrive (''3500, 2572'') |
| Elara Voss, League Registrar | Registering with the [[:uo:systems:league|League]], your rank, the Compact's Mule errand | The League field office (''3459, 2601'') |
| The requisitions board and an Appraiser | Turning in [[:uo:systems:requisitions|requisitions]], selling magic items | The League field office |
| The eighteen guildmasters | Joining and working for the [[:uo:systems:guilds|guilds]] | Each guild's hall; ''[guild'' and "Show me the way" points you to the nearest |
| Wren, the Thieves' Den lookout | Points the way to the Thieves' guildmaster | By the fighting pit |
| The Reset Stone | A floating, shattered runestone | The town |
| The ankh by the Registrar | Resurrection for ghosts | Beside the League field office |

The New Haven training board offers the 26 skill-training quests from the tile you arrive on.

===== Seasons =====

From December 1 to January 6 New Haven dresses for winter: a Christmas tree with presents and Father Christmas in the square, candy cane arches at the gates, wreaths and bells on the lamp posts, a feast and a fireplace at the Bountiful Harvest Inn, and a sleigh on the pier.

===== Nearby =====

  * **Old Haven**, the ruins to the south-east, with Vorlak the Impaler and the Cleansing Old Haven quest.
  * **The Rootdeep Burrows** under Old Haven, still being dug: see [[:uo:drafts:rootdeep_burrows|the draft page]].
