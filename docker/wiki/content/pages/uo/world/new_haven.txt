====== New Haven ======

{{:uo:media:new_haven_route.svg?nolink&700|New Haven route diagram}}

New Haven is the starting town: the guildmasters, the League's field office and the trainers are all here.

The New Haven training board offers the 26 skill-training quests from the tile you arrive on.

===== Seasons =====

From December 1 to January 6 New Haven dresses for winter: a lit Christmas tree, garlands, candles and presents in the bank, small lit trees at the bank's doors, on the square and along the main roads, snow here and there, and an advent calendar on the bank's wall with a small gift behind each day's door, one door a day in December. See [[seasonal|Seasonal decorations]].

===== Old Haven, the training ground =====

Old Haven is an apprentice training ground again, as on the official shards. While you are on a New Haven trainer's quest that sends you there (Magic Resist, the fighting skills, Magery, Healing and the others), you learn that skill **two to four times as fast** in the ruins, until the quest ends at 50. Your trainer's words and an **Accelerated Skillgain** icon tell you when you are in the right place.

Slaying 50 creatures in the ruins earns the achievement **Old Haven's Least Welcome Regular**.
