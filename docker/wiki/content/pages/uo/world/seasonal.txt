====== Seasonal Decorations ======

Some decorations go up for a season and come down again by themselves. The dates are the shard's own (US Central time). These decorations are Shattered Legacy's own.

===== Halloween: October 24 to November 2 =====

Pumpkins, carved pumpkins, lanterns, candles, cobwebs, bones and the odd cauldron or scarecrow appear around the main banks:

  * **Trammel**: New Haven, Britain (west bank), Moonglow, Trinsic, Vesper, Minoc, Yew, Skara Brae and Jhelom.
  * **Felucca**: the same banks but New Haven's.
  * **Malas**: Luna and Umbra.
  * **Tokuno**: Zento.
  * **Ter Mur**: the Royal City.

They are part of the town: you cannot pick them up, and they are gone on November 3.

While the decorations are up, **[[sorcerers_dungeon|Treasures of Sorcerer's Dungeon]]** runs in Ilshenar: undead, nine holiday bosses and Jack the Pumpkin King.

===== Winter in New Haven: December 1 to January 6 =====

New Haven dresses up for winter:

  * A lit Christmas tree, garlands, candles and presents in the bank.
  * Small lit trees at the bank's doors, on the square and along the main roads, and snow here and there.
  * An **advent calendar** on the bank's wall, with a small gift behind each day's door, one door a day in December.

The decorations stay up through January 6 and are gone the next day. The winter art comes with the player package, so update your game through the launcher. See [[new_haven|New Haven]].
