====== Seasonal Decorations ======

Some decorations go up for a season and come down again by themselves. The dates are the shard's own (US Central time). These decorations are Shattered Legacy's own.

===== Halloween: October 24 to November 2 =====

Pumpkins, carved pumpkins, lanterns, candles, cobwebs, bones and the odd cauldron or scarecrow appear around the main banks:

  * **Trammel**: New Haven, Britain (west bank), Moonglow, Trinsic, Vesper, Minoc, Yew, Skara Brae and Jhelom.
  * **Felucca**: the same banks but New Haven's.
  * **Malas**: Luna and Umbra.
  * **Tokuno**: Zento.
  * **Ter Mur**: the Royal City.

They are part of the town: you cannot pick them up, and they are gone on November 3.

===== Winter in New Haven: December 1 to January 6 =====

New Haven dresses for winter: a Christmas tree with presents and Father Christmas in the square, candy cane arches at the gates, wreaths and bells on the lamp posts, a feast and a fireplace at the Bountiful Harvest Inn, and a sleigh on the pier. See [[new_haven|New Haven]].
