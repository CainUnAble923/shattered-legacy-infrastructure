====== The Test Crystal ======

**Draft: test shard only.** Both crystals on this page exist so play testers can try things without the grind. Neither works on the live shard: the Test Crystal deletes itself if it ever reaches one.

===== The Test Crystal (skill gain) =====

The test shard trains at the real shard's rates. Every tester gets a **Test Crystal** in their bank instead; double-click it to set your own skill gain chance to **Off, 2x, 5x, 10x or 25x**. It affects only you, is saved with your character, and glows gold while it is on.

  * Below 10 skill you gain on every use anyway, so the crystal changes nothing there.
  * With the crystal Off, your gains are exactly what the live shard would give. Turn it Off before you test how a skill feels.
  * If your bank has none, ask staff for one.

===== The Dev Testing Crystal (costs) =====

Staff can also hand a tester a **Dev Testing Crystal**. Double-click it to open a small menu and turn it on (green) or off (red). While it is on:

  * Guild work orders (the Miners' Compact, the Society of Smiths and every other guild) skip their material, voucher, gold and ingot costs and their skill and standing checks.
  * Ordinary crafting skips its skill and material requirements.
  * Restoring and upgrading a Jacob's pickaxe costs nothing.

It does **not** skip registry unlocks or guild membership: you still have to join a guild and unlock a thing the normal way, so the steps themselves get tested. It is blessed, so it cannot be dropped or looted.

===== The Test Kit =====

New test characters also get a **Shattered Legacy Test Kit** in their bank: a bag with a stack of every shard resource.
