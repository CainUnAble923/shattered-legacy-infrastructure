====== The Test Crystal ======

**Draft: test shard only.** The Test Crystal exists so play testers can try guild and crafting systems without grinding for the materials. It is never handed out on the live shard.

===== What it does =====

A **Dev Testing Crystal** sits in a tester's backpack. Double-click it to open a small menu and turn it on (green) or off (red).

While it is **on**:

  * Guild work orders (the Miners' Compact, the Society of Smiths and every other guild) skip their material, voucher, gold and ingot costs and their skill and standing checks.
  * Ordinary crafting skips its skill and material requirements.
  * Restoring and upgrading a Jacob's pickaxe costs nothing.

It does **not** skip registry unlocks or guild membership: you still have to join a guild and unlock a thing the normal way, so the steps themselves get tested.

While it is **off** the crystal does nothing. It is blessed, so it cannot be dropped or looted.

===== Getting one =====

Ask staff on the test shard. New test characters also get a **Shattered Legacy Test Kit** in their bank: a bag with a stack of every shard resource, for the same reason.
