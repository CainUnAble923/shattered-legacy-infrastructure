====== The Rideable Red Dragon ======

**Draft: players cannot get one yet.** Staff are testing a rideable red dragon, and a dragon that can take any color, on the test shard. Players cannot tame, buy or earn one. For now it rides like a horse, with a horse's numbers, until it is decided what it should be and how players will earn it. This page will fill in when that is settled.

It is unique to Shattered Legacy.
