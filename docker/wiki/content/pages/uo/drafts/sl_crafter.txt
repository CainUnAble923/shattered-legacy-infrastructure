====== Crafter trainer (SL_Crafter.py) ======

**Draft: this script ships when Chase publishes it.** It is written and runs on his PC, but it is not in the player package's Legion scripts yet. Nothing on this page can be downloaded until then; see [[:uo:macros:start|Macros]] for the scripts that are.

SL_Crafter trains the eight crafts that take [[:uo:professions:bulk_orders|bulk orders]]: Blacksmithy, Tailoring, Carpentry, Bowcraft/Fletching, Tinkering, Cooking, Alchemy and Inscription. It reads your craft menu, picks the item that teaches you most for what it costs, crafts it in batches of 25 with the shard's **Craft X**, recycles what it made, fills your bulk order deeds with it, and fetches more materials from a chest or your bank when it runs low.

===== What you need =====

^ Item ^ Why ^
| The craft's tools | Tongs, sewing kit, saw, fletcher's tools, tinker's tools, a skillet, a mortar and pestle, a scribe's pen; and the craft's workplace where one is needed (a forge and anvil for smithing). |
| Materials | In your pack, in bags if you like; it opens them. |
| A storage chest or a banker | **Set Storage** and target it. A restock trip fetches four batches' worth and comes back. |
| A trash barrel | Optional. **Set Trash** lets it throw away what it cannot recycle or put in a deed. |
| A salvage bag or scissors | Optional. Smiths get ingots back, tailors cut cloth back; everything else goes to the deeds or the trash. |

===== Setting it up =====

  - Pick **Setup for:** "All crafts (shared)" to use one spot, storage and trash for every craft, or a craft to give it its own.
  - **Set Work Spot** where you craft (by your forge, your loom, your bank).
  - **Set Storage**: your material chest, or a banker for your bank box.
  - Optionally **Add BOD Giver**: the vendor or guild master you take bulk orders from.
  - Press **Start**. The first run of a craft reads its whole menu once; **Rescan menu** reads it again after you learn new patterns.

===== The control window =====

  * **Start / Pause / Stop**.
  * **Setup for:** the dropdown above, with **Set Work Spot**, **Clear Spot**, **Set Storage**, **Add BOD Giver** and **Clear Givers**.
  * **Pick for:** Fastest, Balanced or Cheapest. Fastest takes the item with the best gain chance whatever it costs; Cheapest the one that costs least per gain; Balanced sits between.
  * **Rescan menu**.
  * **Bank BOD points (lossy)**: off by default. On, finished deeds bank their reward points instead of asking.

When nothing left in the menu can teach you, it stops and lists the best items that would, and which material each is short.

===== What it does between batches =====

  * Reads the **Craft X** summary ("Made N in M attempts") and re-picks the item.
  * **Bulk orders**: fills small deeds first, combines a bag of items into a deed, then puts finished small deeds into large ones. With a BOD giver set, when a giver's timer is up it walks there, hands finished deeds in and asks for new ones until it is refused.
  * **Recycling**: a smith's salvage bag or a smelt; a tailor's scissors; the rest to the trash barrel.
  * **Restocking**: walks to the storage spot, fetches four batches of the materials it needs, walks back. It walks across town and through teleporters, up to about 400 tiles.

===== What it never does =====

  * It never sells to a vendor: Legion scripts cannot use a shop's sell list.
  * It never takes League commissions.
  * It never crafts something your menu does not show. Patterns you have not learned are not there to pick (see [[:uo:professions:recipes|Recipes]]).
  * It never leaves your work spot except for a restock or a bulk order trip, and it walks back.

===== Known limits =====

  * **Past 100, only Blacksmithy, Tinkering, Carpentry and Fletching keep climbing**, by crafting in the shard's own metals and woods (Platinum from 112.5 Blacksmithy, Ironwood from 100 Carpentry; see [[:uo:professions:materials|Metals and woods]]). **Tailoring, Alchemy, Inscription and Cooking have no path past their highest stock item yet.**
  * **At 100 Blacksmithy the menu can go quiet**: the items between 100 and 120 are Band V patterns, learned one scroll at a time, and the Platinum ceiling only starts at 112.5. Learn Band V scrolls at the Society of Smiths (rank 4 and up) or the menu's hint line will tell you where.
  * Inscription batches are capped by your mana.
  * Bulk order gathering and turn-in are built but not yet proven on the test shard.
  * It writes ''SL_Crafter.log'' next to the script. Send that along if you report a problem.
