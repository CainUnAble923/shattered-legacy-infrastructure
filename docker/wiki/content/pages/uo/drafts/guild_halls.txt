====== The Guilds' Main Halls ======

**Draft: test shard only.** The halls below stand on the test shard so they can be walked and checked. They are not in the Guild Directory or "Show me the way" yet, and every guild still meets at its New Haven chapter. This page shows where each guild's main hall is planned, so the list may change before the halls open.

Each guild keeps its New Haven chapter when its main hall opens. A hall has the guild's sign, its guildmaster inside, and decor in the guild's style. Where a hall has a tower, it will be walkable once the hall is built.
