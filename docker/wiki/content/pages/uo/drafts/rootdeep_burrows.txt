====== The Rootdeep Burrows ======

**Draft: not open to players.** The Burrows' route, rules, creatures and Vault are built, but the dungeon is placed in the world by staff, and that placement is not confirmed yet. This page describes what is coming; do not go looking for the entrance yet.

The Rootdeep Burrows is Shattered Legacy's own starter dungeon, dug under the ruins of **Old Haven**, with its entrance at the hole in the ruined Second Bank of Haven, reached through the bank's collapsed south wall. A rope ladder inside takes you back up. It is unique to this shard.

===== The rules =====

  * **No magical entry.** No spell or scroll takes you into the Burrows, and no rune can be marked or gate opened inside them. Walk in, and recall or walk out.
  * **Young players** are not safe here: monsters can attack them. No more than **three** monsters at once can set on one Young player, but that only stops a crowd, not a hard fight.
  * **New characters** can come here before they leave New Haven: under the tutorial's island rule, the Burrows count as part of the island.
  * A working **ankh** stands at the landing for resurrection.

===== The zones =====

Shallow to deep:

  * **The Vault**, a tutorial-only room beside the landing: only a character in the tutorial (and their pet) can walk in. Its boss is **The Overdraft**, a living bank strongbox. When it dies, everyone who fought it (pets count) gets credit and a one-way crawlway opens into the Warren for two minutes. Once you have that credit, or have graduated from the tutorial, you cannot go back into the Vault. Decoy chest mimics teach that some chests have teeth.
  * **The Warren**: burrow grubs, burrow scamps and redcap scamps, under **The Burrow Mother**. Burrowhide leather.
  * **The Tangle**: rootlings, sporecaps and bramblehulks. Rootwood.
  * **The Dawnseam**: dawnshards and seamguards. Dawnstone ore.
  * **The Gloom**: gloomlings, gloomspinners and gloomweavers, under **The Gloom Queen**. Gloomsilk cloth.

Around the tutorial farm above: gourdlings, crop crows, burrow scamp slingers, the cackler pack and the burrow hog.

===== What it gives =====

  * Four **starter materials** for crafters around 45 skill, found only here: Dawnstone ore and Rootwood, and Burrowhide leather and Gloomsilk cloth from its creatures. See [[:uo:professions:materials|Metals and woods]].
  * Each creature family drops its **part** (burrower claws, rootheart sap, dawnstone shards, gloom glands); the hog and the grub carve Burrowhide; the bosses drop jewelry and a boss part.
  * The Thieves' Den lets an Initiate steal from the Burrows' creatures, and a Master from its bosses. See [[:uo:professions:stealing|Stealing from monsters]].
  * A requisition Lead can point here from the start. See [[:uo:systems:requisitions|Requisitions]].
