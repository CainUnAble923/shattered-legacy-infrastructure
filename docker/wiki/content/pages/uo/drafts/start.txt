====== Drafts ======

Pages here describe things that are **on the test shard only**, not yet finished, or not yet published. They are kept out of the sidebar so a player does not go looking for something they cannot use yet. Each page says at the top why it is a draft.

  * [[tutorial|The New Haven tutorial]]: the nine core trials, the wings, the farm and graduation; on the test shard only, with entry turned off elsewhere
  * [[levels_and_loops|Levels and loops]]: what a loop is today, and the level and loop ladder that is still planned
  * [[companions|Companions and evo pets]]: the tutorial's starter pets and the planned companions
  * [[guild_halls|The guilds' main halls]]: placed on the test shard only so far
  * [[world_map|The world map: Sosaria]]: Trammel and Felucca's new land, from the day of the Siege of Sosaria swap; not live yet
  * [[rootdeep_burrows|The Rootdeep Burrows]]: the dungeon under Old Haven; built, but not open to players yet
  * [[levelable_gear|Levelable crafted gear]]: built, but the Smith's Seal has no player source yet
  * [[red_dragon|The rideable red dragon]]: staff are trying it; players cannot get one yet
  * [[test_crystal|The Test Crystal]]: the test shard's skill gain booster, and the staff testing crystal
  * [[sl_mage|Mage trainer (SL_Mage.py)]]: a Legion script, written but not yet published
  * [[sl_crafter|Crafter trainer (SL_Crafter.py)]]: a Legion script, written but not yet published

When a system goes live, its page moves to its proper namespace and gets a sidebar link.
