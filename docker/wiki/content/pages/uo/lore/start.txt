====== Lore Hub ======

This namespace is for shard fiction, faction flavor, place histories, and rumors.

===== Good First Lore Pages =====

  * Shattered Legacy premise
  * New Haven rumors
  * League of Extraordinary Citizens
  * Miners' Compact
  * Notable player deeds

===== Writing Style =====

Keep lore pages link-heavy:

  * link places to [[:uo:world:start|World]]
  * link factions to related professions
  * link rumors to quests or NPCs
  * add images when a place or person matters
