# Compact Mule

## Purpose

The Compact Mule is a future Miners' Compact reward line and logistics companion.

It should be a rideable beast of burden for miners, surveyors, and expedition crews. It is not intended to be a primary combat pet. Its role is to survive hostile mining excursions, carry substantially more than a normal pack horse, and support expedition logistics through guild-linked utility upgrades.

## Core Design

The Compact Mule should:

- be rideable
- function as a pack animal
- hold substantially more items and weight than a normal pack horse
- survive hostile mining excursions better than a normal pack animal
- visually fit Ultima Online and Shattered Legacy's grounded tone
- eventually support breeding and bloodlines
- act as a platform for cross-guild utility modules

If possible, the base mobile can use the pack horse or similar pack animal mobile with a different hue and custom name.

## Design Rule

```text
The mule's job is to survive, carry, and support expeditions — not to replace combat pets.
```

Combat strength should be defensive and survival-focused. Utility and logistics are the core identity.

## Tier Philosophy

The mule should not have one tier for every ore. Instead, it should use 4–5 meaningful tiers that combine animal breeding, training, barding, and magical/technical improvements.

Hue choices should look natural on an animal first and ore-inspired second.

Good hue inspirations:

- Dull Copper: earthy brown / rust
- Bronze: warm brown / bronze
- Shadow Iron: charcoal / dark grey
- Valorite: muted blue-grey accents
- Adamantine / Celestial: late-game magical or legendary hues

## Suggested Tier Ladder

| Tier | Name | Hue Theme | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Compact Mule | natural / dull copper-brown | basic rideable pack mule |
| 2 | Bronzeback Mule | bronze / copper-brown | stronger hauler |
| 3 | Shadowhide Mule | shadow iron / charcoal | cave and hostile-zone hauler |
| 4 | Valorite-Barded Mule | muted valorite blue-grey | dangerous expedition mule |
| 5 | Celestial or Adamantine Mule | celestial pale / adamantine deep metal | legendary logistics companion |

Names can be adjusted later, but the tier identity should remain:

```text
basic hauler → heavy hauler → hostile-zone hauler → expedition survivor → legendary logistics beast
```

## Tier Scaling

Progression should primarily improve:

- carry weight
- item capacity
- hit points
- stamina
- resistances
- survivability
- module slots
- hostile-zone reliability

Suggested module slot scaling:

| Mule Tier | Module Slots |
| --- | --- |
| Tier 1 | 1 |
| Tier 2 | 2 |
| Tier 3 | 3 |
| Tier 4 | 4 |
| Tier 5 | all modules / no practical module lockout |

Top-tier mules should eventually be able to install all major utility modules. Design module limits and UI with this scaling in mind.

## Acquisition

The base Compact Mule should be purchasable or unlockable through the Miners' Compact.

Potential requirements:

- Miners' Compact rank
- Mining Vouchers
- gold
- ore or ingot supply contracts
- future Animal Husbandry / Stablemaster support

Higher tiers should require higher Compact rank, more vouchers, rare materials, and cross-guild upgrade components.

## Cross-Guild Module System

The Compact Mule should become a visible example of Shattered Legacy's interwoven guild economy.

The Miners' Compact provides the base animal and mining identity. Other guilds provide specialized upgrades.

```text
Compact Mule = base logistics companion
Guild upgrades = specialized utility modules
Breeding = future bloodline/trait layer
```

## Suggested Modules

| Module | Guild Tie-In | Function |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Compact Field Smelter Rig | Miners + Smiths | smelt ore carried by the mule |
| Arcane Ward Harness | Mage Guild | defensive magic and environmental resistance |
| Clockwork Load Harness | Tinkers | more storage, sorting, mechanical support |
| Survey Saddle Roll | Cartographers | survey storage, route logging, future map hooks |
| Trailwise Tack | Rangers | stamina, wilderness survival, movement reliability |
| Field Medic Pack | Healers | bandage storage, self-heal or emergency stabilize |
| Expedition Barding | Warriors / Mercenaries / Smiths | survivability and damage reduction |
| Alchemical Feed Satchel | Alchemists | temporary buffs, cure/protection feed |
| Breeding Tack | Animal Husbandry / Stablemasters | future breeding and bloodline support |

## Compact Field Smelter Rig

A portable mule-mounted smelting rig.

Potential requirements:

- Miners' Compact standing
- Society of Smiths standing
- Mining Vouchers
- Smithing currency or seals
- ingots, forge components, fuel, and heat-resistant fittings

Behavior:

```text
Allows the mule to smelt ore carried in its pack.
```

Balance:

- slower than a stationary forge
- may consume fuel or charges
- may have lower efficiency at low tier
- may improve with Mining and Blacksmithing skill
- should not replace guild halls, houses, or permanent forge bonuses

## Arcane Ward Harness

A mage-crafted defensive harness.

Potential requirements:

- Mage Guild / Guild of Arcane Arts standing
- Miners' Compact standing
- reagents
- inscription/rune components
- Arcane Research Notes or equivalent guild currency

Behavior options:

- self-only magic resistance ward
- elemental hazard protection
- occasional self-cure or self-heal
- pack-protection ward
- emergency stable/recall-style extraction at top tier

This should remain utility/survival focused, not offensive spellcasting.

## Clockwork Load Harness

A Tinker/Engineer upgrade for load management.

Potential requirements:

- Tinkers Guild standing
- Mining Vouchers
- Tinker currency or engineering components
- gears, springs, ingots

Behavior options:

- increased item capacity
- increased weight capacity
- optional auto-sort categories
- module maintenance support
- future Clockwork Scavenger integration

## Survey Saddle Roll

A Cartography-focused upgrade.

Potential requirements:

- Cartographers Guild standing
- Miners' Compact standing
- maps, scrolls, survey copies

Behavior options:

- stores survey maps and copied log entries
- supports route recording
- future ClassicUO marker/export integration
- future ore survey map support

## Trailwise Tack

A Ranger-focused travel and survival upgrade.

Potential requirements:

- Rangers Guild standing
- leather, hides, trail rations
- wilderness trophies

Behavior options:

- improved stamina
- better movement endurance
- reduced panic/flee behavior
- better wilderness survival
- out-of-combat regeneration

## Field Medic Pack

A Healer-focused support module.

Potential requirements:

- Healers Guild standing
- bandages
- cure potions
- Healer's Marks or equivalent guild currency

Behavior options:

- stores bandages and potions
- slow mule self-heal outside combat
- emergency stabilize once per cooldown
- future bonded mule recovery support

## Expedition Barding

A hostile-zone survival module.

Potential requirements:

- Warriors Guild or Mercenary Guild standing
- Smithing support
- armor plates and rare ingots

Behavior options:

- increased HP
- increased resistances
- reduced incoming damage
- reduced chance to be targeted if feasible

This should not turn the mule into a warhorse. It should allow the mule to survive while the player handles threats.

## Alchemical Feed Satchel

An Alchemist-focused buff module.

Potential requirements:

- Alchemist standing
- reagents
- bottles
- crafted tonics or feed

Behavior options:

- consumes special feed or tonics for temporary mule buffs
- poison resistance
- fire resistance
- stamina recovery
- emergency cure

## Breeding Future

The mule should eventually integrate with the future animal breeding system.

Possible bloodline traits:

| Trait | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Burdened Bloodline | higher carry weight |
| Stoneback | physical resistance |
| Emberblood | fire resistance or smelting synergy |
| Nightmare-Kin | magic resistance / courage |
| Surefooted | stamina and movement reliability |
| Deepdelver | dungeon survivability |
| Celestial Tempered | rare legendary line |

Lore can justify high-tier magical traits through carefully controlled crossbreeding or arcane conditioning using creatures such as fire steeds or nightmares.

## Guild Work Order Integration

Mule modules should create cross-guild demand through Guild Work Orders.

Examples:

```text
Smiths Guild Work Order:
Forge Field Smelter Plates for the Miners' Compact.
```

```text
Mage Guild Work Order:
Prepare Arcane Ward Harness components for Compact expedition mules.
```

```text
Tinkers Guild Work Order:
Assemble Clockwork Load Harness components.
```

```text
Rangers Guild Work Order:
Prepare Trailwise Tack kits from cured leather.
```

Players in other guilds should be able to gain their own guild reputation by supplying mule upgrade components.

## Phase Placement

This is a future reward line, not part of Phase 2 verification.

Potential implementation phase:

- Phase 3/4 design and reward planning
- actual implementation after the Miners' Compact and Guild Work Order framework are stable
- breeding integration later

## Open Balance Questions

To decide before implementation:

- exact carry weight and item count per tier
- whether mule is bonded by default
- whether mule death drops pack contents or protects them
- whether mule modules are removable or permanent
- whether module installation is account-bound, mule-bound, or item-based
- whether top-tier mules can install every module simultaneously or need an unlock to do so
- how much combat targeting protection is acceptable
